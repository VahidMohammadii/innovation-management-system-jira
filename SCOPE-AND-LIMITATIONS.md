# Scope & Limitations

I built this project as an **independent Jira Cloud portfolio simulation**, not as a production rollout. Its purpose is to demonstrate how I translate innovation governance into configurable work items, automation, evidence and management reporting.

## Included

- A conceptual four-stage, three-gate innovation roadmap and documented Jira work items.
- Eight described automation controls, selected observed rule behavior and a six-gadget governance dashboard.
- Published screenshots, audit references, issue History and a transparent record of AI assistance and my own verification.

## Boundaries

- The dashboard figures and rule outcomes are **simulation observations**, not organizational KPIs or measured business impact.
- The published evidence supports the specific documented scenarios, not exhaustive testing of every branch, role or permission configuration.
- The original integration-test records are being updated; remaining case-level evidence will be added in a subsequent project iteration. See [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md).
- The [rule catalog](03-automation-rules/automation-rule-catalog.md) and [workflow diagram](01-system-design/conceptual-stage-gate-flow.md) are **documentation**, not machine-readable exports of the current Jira configuration. See [config](config/README.md).
- The proposed municipal and Mittelstand adoption models are **transferability analyses**, not client implementations.

I remain responsible for the Jira configuration, tests, evidence and conclusions. AI was used as a support tool, not as an independent auditor.
